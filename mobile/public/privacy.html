Privacy policy

A clear look at the information behind your workout, your saved plans and your conversations with Mat.

Effective October 20, 2026 · Applies to the Spotter app and Mat voice coach

Google helps you sign in. Spotter saves your workout information. If you turn on voice, ElevenLabs processes your conversation and workout context. For privacy questions or deletion requests, email Thanmay.

01. Information we collect

  • Account information: when you sign in with Google, Spotter receives your Google account identifier, name and email address, and uses a sign-in token to verify your identity. Spotter does not receive your Google password or request access to Gmail, Drive, contacts or calendars.
  • Workout and preference information: selected muscles and exercises, plans and weekly schedules, sets, planned repetitions, entered weights, set durations, rest times, equipment reports, session actions and workout history. We also store preferences such as coaching style, language, exclusions, trainer name/avatar and any personal motivation note you choose to enter.
  • Voice interactions: if you enable voice and grant microphone permission, audio and conversation content are processed by ElevenLabs and the services it uses to provide the voice coach. Spotter sends workout context, including your display name, preferences, current session and, when enabled, your personal motivation reminder. Avoid sharing information you do not want processed by a voice service.
  • Technical information: authentication/session tokens and action identifiers support sign-in, synchronization and reliable workout updates. Hosting and service providers may process IP addresses, browser/device information, request timestamps and diagnostic logs when delivering the service.

02. How we use information

We use information to authenticate you, save and restore your profile and plans, track completed workout actions, provide exercise guidance and voice responses, synchronize connected controls, maintain security, diagnose failures and respond to support or privacy requests.

Workout repetitions and equipment availability are entered or reported by you; Spotter does not detect gym occupancy, observe your form, or automatically count repetitions.

03. Service providers and sharing

Information is processed by providers as needed to operate Spotter:

  • Google: account sign-in and identity verification. Google Privacy Policy.
  • ElevenLabs: voice conversations, audio processing, generated speech and conversation tools. Audio and transcripts may be retained according to the agent configuration and provider policies; this policy does not promise zero retention or exclusion from model improvement. ElevenLabs Privacy Policy.
  • Vercel and Neon: hosted application/API delivery and PostgreSQL storage for hosted account and workout records. Local development may use SQLite instead. Vercel Privacy Policy · Neon Privacy Policy.
  • Optional Snowflake analytics: if the operator enables the separate batch export, selected workout events can be exported with pseudonymous session/event identifiers, exercise details, timestamps and set metrics. That export excludes account profiles, email, authentication tokens, personal motivation notes and transcripts. It is not an automatic part of every hosted workout. Snowflake Privacy Notice.

We do not sell personal information or use Google account data for targeted advertising. We may disclose information when required by applicable law or when necessary to protect the service and its users. Providers may process data in countries other than your own.

04. Browser storage and microphone controls

The web app uses session storage for sign-in and workout access tokens, and local storage to remember whether an opening reminder was already delivered. These support app functionality. Clearing browser storage removes local access information but does not delete server records.

You can decline or revoke microphone permission in your browser or device settings and use available button controls. Ending the voice conversation stops the app’s active conversation; a mute indicator alone should not be treated as proof that hardware microphone access has ended.

This policy webpage contains no sign-in, advertising scripts, analytics scripts or forms. Its hosting provider may still process technical request information.

05. Retention and deletion

Spotter retains saved profiles, plans, workout sessions and action history to provide the service. The current application does not implement an automatic retention deadline or a self-service account-deletion control. Records remain until removed by the operator or the relevant storage provider’s retention process.

To request access, correction or deletion of your information, email mdasthanmay@gmail.com, preferably from your sign-in email address. We may verify your identity before acting. Tell us whether your request includes voice records or optional analytics exports so those copies can be addressed where applicable. Backups, provider records and information required for security or legal obligations may remain subject to their applicable retention limits.

You can edit available profile and workout preferences in the app. You can also remove Spotter’s Google connection through your Google Account connections; this does not by itself delete records already held by Spotter.

06. Security

Spotter uses authentication tokens and server-side validation to restrict access and protect workout state. Hosted connections use HTTPS. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Keep session credentials and pairing information private, especially on shared devices.

07. Children and fitness information

Spotter is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information, contact us to request its removal. Workout and motivation information can be personal; enter only what you are comfortable sharing. Spotter provides general fitness guidance and is not a medical service.

08. Updates and contact

We may update this policy when the service or its data practices change. The effective date at the top identifies the current version.

Spotter is a developer-built workout application with Mat as its default voice coach. Privacy contact: Thanmay Mohandas Das, mdasthanmay@gmail.com.
